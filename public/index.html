The account that was losing money

IshqME, a D2C fashion and lifestyle brand. Performance Analyst, Growth and Paid Media at M1 Marketing Pvt Ltd, January 2025 to now.

When I took over IshqME's ad account it was returning ₹0.80 for every rupee spent, below break-even. I introduced structured creative angle testing, then wrote influencer scripts around the angles that won.

ROAS reached 2 within six months and sits around 3 today, with CAC falling steadily along the way.

I own the ₹15L+ monthly Meta and Google budget end to end: funnel-stage media plans, plus bid and budget strategy across acquisition and retargeting.

I inherited a 0.8 ROAS. Six months later it was 2.

Half of customers now come back

Repeat purchase went from 27% to 51% at IshqME, and the average basket grew from ₹3,000 to ₹3,700.

I segmented existing customers by how recently, how often and how much they buy (RFM), then built automated email and WhatsApp journeys for each segment.

For bigger baskets I launched product bundles and a progressive cart-value offer panel that unlocks tiered discounts as the cart grows.
